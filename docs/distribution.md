# Historical Viewer source acquisition

The matching preferred/corresponding source and original notices for every preserved release are available without an account or charge in the [public source archive](https://github.com/menaje/2d-cad-viewer-source-archive).

Choose your exact version in the [version index](../README.md). Viewer/MPL source snapshots include source files, build scripts and lockfiles. Every LibreDWG converter is paired with its original platform-specific GPL source-complete archive, including patches, adapter source, build scripts, licenses and checksums.

[Exact artifact-to-source mapping](https://github.com/menaje/2d-cad-viewer-source-archive/blob/main/manifest.json) and [archive SHA-256 checksums](https://github.com/menaje/2d-cad-viewer-source-archive/blob/main/SHA256SUMS) identify the preserved bytes. This is the replacement source-acquisition path for historical files. No new binary version is published; old installation/update URLs may stop working without changing recipients' source rights.

For a missing download, [request source](https://github.com/menaje/2d-cad-viewer/issues/new) with version, platform and filename. Direct acquisition requires no account or payment and has no planned expiry.
